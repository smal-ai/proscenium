# A small lift

## Canonical brief and treatment

A silent, two-shot paper-kite concept for a quiet 6-second desktop storyboard.
Warm-paper texture, warm ochre paper and a slate string sit against a soft sky.
No people, dialogue, cast, industrial subjects or sound are proposed.
The small lift and subsequent settling should feel gentle rather than dramatic.

## Proposed production timing, not an authored timeline

Each shot proposes 3 seconds at 24 fps (72 production frames per shot;
144 frames / 6 seconds overall). These are production intentions, not
review-card pacing, source-frame assignments or an already-authored timeline.
No scene exists. Both shots therefore keep scene_version, source_range and fps
null until actual scene, timeline and camera bindings exist.

- kite-rise: over the proposed 3 seconds at 24 fps, a low, lightly tilted paper
  kite rises into open sky. Propose a gentle counterclockwise camera arc during ascent,
  viewed from above the subject. Start low, pass through a lifted intermediate
  composition, and end higher with generous sky around the kite.
- kite-rest: over the proposed 3 seconds at 24 fps, retain a locked camera while
  the kite settles from the lift into a quiet hover. Start at the lifted pose,
  ease downward with a small string sway, and end almost still.
